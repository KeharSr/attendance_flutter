# Privacy Policy for GBN Store HRMS

Last updated: 16 November 2026

GBN Store HRMS is an employee attendance and human resources application operated by [OPERATOR / LEGAL COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how we handle personal information when you use the app.

## Information we collect

- Account information: your name, phone number, employee/user ID, and account role, provided by you or your employer. We process your password when you sign in and use authentication tokens to maintain your session.
- Employment records: attendance dates, clock-in and clock-out times, working hours, leave requests, overtime requests, missing-attendance requests, reasons you submit, approval decisions, and administrator comments.
- Location information: with your permission, we obtain your device's precise latitude, longitude, and location accuracy when you initiate clock-in or clock-out. These details are sent to our server to verify and record attendance. The app does not continuously track your location or collect location in the background.
- Notification information: Firebase installation identifiers and notification content needed to deliver attendance reminders and request updates. Google Firebase may also process technical service information, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure its services.
- Information stored on your device: basic profile details, sign-in state, authentication tokens, notification preferences, installation identifiers, and received notifications.

## How we use information

We use this information to authenticate employees, verify and record attendance, manage leave and overtime, correct missing attendance, display records and request decisions, send work-related notifications, and maintain account security and app operation.

## Who receives information

Your employer's authorized HR staff and administrators may access information needed to manage employment records and requests. Our backend and hosting providers process information to operate the service. We use Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications; Firebase processes installation identifiers and message content for this purpose. See [Privacy and Security in Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Firebase may process information in countries outside your own.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate fraud or protect the service and its users.

## Permissions and your choices

Location permission is used for attendance verification. Denying or revoking it prevents location-based clock-in and clock-out. Notification permission enables reminders and request updates; denying it does not prevent sign-in. You can manage these permissions in your device settings. Notifications may appear on your lock screen depending on your device settings.

## Retention and deletion

We retain account and employment records only for as long as needed to administer employment, meet applicable recordkeeping obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service. The required period may vary by record type and applicable law. Contact us for the retention period applicable to your records.

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information or account by emailing [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] or contacting your employer's HR administrator. Include the app name and your employee ID or registered phone number; do not send your password. We may verify your identity before processing a request. Records that must be retained for legal obligations or legitimate security purposes may be kept for those purposes; we will explain applicable limits when responding.

Signing out removes the app's saved sign-in credentials and basic profile information from that device. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete employment records held on our server.

## Security

The app requires authenticated access to employee services and uses platform secure storage for authentication tokens on your device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device and sign-in credentials protected, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized account access.

## Children

This app is intended for authorized employees and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or our practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent.
